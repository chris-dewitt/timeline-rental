# Literary Voice — Timeline Rental

Same DNA as Superposition Sessions. Different costume.

---

## Narrator modes

| Mode | When | Style |
|------|------|-------|
| **Inner** | Walking, observing | Stream of consciousness, edited tight |
| **Examiner** | Dialogue scenes | Calm, surgical, unsettling questions |
| **Collapse** | Observation events | Past tense shift — "the timeline closed" |
| **Clerk** | Store hub | Detached, knows too much, one sentence |

---

## Rules

1. Original copy only — no movie quote fan service
2. Present tense for inner monologue; collapse can snap to past
3. Specific sensory detail — rain, neon, wet cardboard, bad coffee
4. Instagram-age rot: attention, scrolling, false memories — PKD would hate TikTok
5. Short. Dialogue ≤ 2 sentences. Narration ≤ 3.

---

## Collapse narration examples

> three versions of you answered differently. only one gets to be real. the rain doesn't care which.

> the photo chose you. or you chose the photo. causality is a rental — overdue.

> timeline C is dead. in it, you never walked in. the examiner is still waiting there. alone.

---

## Clerk fragments (store hub)

> that tape's been in slot 7 since before you were born. the birth date on your ID disagrees.

> return policy: no refunds across timelines. store credit only.

> someone returned blade runner at 3:14am. it was still inside when we opened.
